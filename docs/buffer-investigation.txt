FINDINGS
1. The final netlist contains 340 sky130_fd_sc_hd__clkdlybuf4s25_1 cells:
   327 fanout buffers and 13 input buffers. These are clock-delay cells used
   as data buffers. Their cell footprint totals about 3,403.3 um2.

2. Post-placement design repair inserted 16 input buffers of this type,
   then 394 repair buffers across 127 nets. Some cells were subsequently
   resized. All eight buffers on the critical rounding path are already
   present after this stage: seven clkdlybuf4s25_1 and one clkbuf_4.
   These seven delay buffers were not inserted by the later hold repair.

3. The separate post-CTS timing pass inserted 267 hold buffers. Every final
   hold-prefixed instance is sky130_fd_sc_hd__dlygate4sd3_1. Their area is
   approximately 2,672.6 um2. The proposed experiment leaves this family
   available. The final timing-repair category totals 677 cells/6,532.5 um2.

4. In max_ss_100C_1v60, 1,709 of 1,770 slew-violating pins (96.6%) lie on
   167 nets driven by clkdlybuf4s25_1. Multiple pins on a net are counted:
   this is NOT 1,709 separate nets or 1,709 delay-buffer instances.
   Worst observed slew is 1.736589 ns at _4351_/A1, on net182 driven by
   fanout182. Its limit is 0.75 ns. All seven capacitance violations are
   outputs of the same cell type, including fanout66 on the critical path.
   Example: fanout182/X has 0.110253 pF load versus a 0.094309 pF limit.
   The library pin limit is tighter than the global 0.2 pF constraint.

5. The checks report additionally lists 17 max-fanout violations: 16 clock
   leaf buffers and one sequential output. These are separate from the
   slew/capacitance totals in the summary and should be tracked explicitly.

6. Automatic buffer selection currently has EXTRA_EXCLUDED_CELLS = null.
   The flow explicitly selected clkdlybuf4s25_1 for input buffering and
   fanout repair. SYNTH_BUFFER_CELL = buf_2 governs synthesis buffering;
   it does not force the physical resizer to choose buf_2. The logs prove
   the selection and insertion stage, but do not expose the internal
   heuristic that preferred this cell. No tool-bug diagnosis is claimed.

ACTIVE EXPERIMENT A -- ONE CONFIGURATION VARIABLE
src/config.json now adds:
  "EXTRA_EXCLUDED_CELLS": ["sky130_fd_sc_hd__clkdlybuf*"]

This excludes the clock-delay-buffer family from synthesis and physical
cell selection using the supported LibreLane variable. Excluding the family
avoids simply substituting another clock-delay variant. Ordinary buf/clkbuf
cells, CTS clock buffers, and dlygate/dlymetal hold-delay cells remain allowed.
RTL, 30 MHz period, four tiles, density and hold margins are unchanged.
The global transition and capacitance limits are not relaxed.

This is a targeted experiment, not a validated fix. Faster data buffers may
expose short paths, causing additional hold repair, and stronger buffers may
increase local area. A new run must establish the net area and timing effects.
Do not project a one-for-one replacement or guaranteed area saving.

Post-CTS timing repair: enabled, 0.1 ns hold margin, 0.05 ns setup margin.
Post-global-routing design repair: disabled.
Post-global-routing timing repair: disabled.
Configured post-route margins (currently inactive): 10% slew/cap;
0.05 ns hold; 0.025 ns setup.
RSZ_CORNERS is unset; repair logs actually read all nine corners. Do not
mistake DEFAULT_CORNER = nom_tt for proof of typical-only repair.
MAX_SLEW_VIOLATION_CORNERS and MAX_CAP_VIOLATION_CORNERS are [""], so the
workflow can finish with electrical warnings. A green workflow alone is not
an acceptance criterion for these experiments.

FOLLOW-UP B -- NOT APPLIED
If A improves buffer selection but leaves routed electrical violations,
merge these overrides into A's src/config.json for a second run:
  "RUN_POST_GRT_DESIGN_REPAIR": true,
  "RUN_POST_GRT_RESIZER_TIMING": true

The first pass addresses routed load/slew using routing estimates; the second
allows setup/hold repair after those modifications. This pairing is deliberate
because fixing slew can create shorter paths. Preserve existing margins and
allow rerouting (GRT_DESIGN_REPAIR_RUN_GRT and GRT_RESIZER_RUN_GRT are already
true). Compare against A rather than changing all settings in one run.
If needed later, investigate corner policy/margins on the remaining nets.
Do not weaken hold checks, raise the 0.75 ns transition limit, or disable
subnormal/rounding functionality to make this experiment appear successful.
After closure, consider enforcing electrical checks at all intended corners.
